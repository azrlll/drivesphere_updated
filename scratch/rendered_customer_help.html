What are the driver license and identification requirements to rent a vehicle?

Renters must present a valid Philippine Professional or Non-Professional Driver's License (held for at least 1 year) or a valid Foreign Driver's License alongside a passport and international driving permit. Two government-issued IDs are required for primary account verification.

Accepted Primary IDs: Passport, UMID, Driver's License, SSS card, or PRC License.
02

How does the security deposit work, and when is it refunded?

Security deposits are held temporarily on your credit card and vary based on the vehicle category (₱5,000 for Sedans, ₱8,000 for SUVs, ₱12,000 for VIP Vans). They are automatically released within 3-5 business days upon a clean vehicle return inspection, assuming zero toll violations or damage.

03

Can I arrange for airport pick-up and drop-off at NAIA, Clark, or Cebu Mactan?

Yes, DriveSphere coordinates white-glove curbside handover at NAIA Terminals 1, 2, 3, Clark International, and Mactan-Cebu. Simply input your flight number during reservation for synchronized terminal bay dispatch.

04

What collision damage coverage and insurance options are included in standard bookings?

Every booking includes standard Compulsory Third-Party Liability (CTPL) and Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) with standard excess caps. You can upgrade to our Premium ₱0 Deductible Protection during checkout for complete indemnity.

05

What should I do in the unlikely event of an accident or mechanical breakdown?

Prioritize your safety and call our 24/7 emergency dispatch hotline immediately at (02) 8888-DRIVE. Our roadside response unit will coordinate traffic management, localized mechanics, and dispatch an instant replacement vehicle within 60 minutes in Metro Manila.
